## Features, as the page describes them

Paraphrased from the page's description:

- Aimed at seven-string players, in the top-of-the-line Type A1 range.
- Stealth looks with a cutaway scoop and full neck access.
- Set-thru neck and body joint, 29" scale, swamp ash body, maple neck, ebony fingerboard.
- 24 stainless steel "super jumbo" frets and Luminlay glow-in-the-dark side dots.
- Evertune constant-tension bridge, Solar 18:1 locking tuners, PPS nut.
- Newly designed Duncan Solar+ high-gain pickups with Alnico VIII magnets.
- Note on the page: this guitar does not fit inside hardcase MCAS.

## Strings and tuning

The page's TUNING field says "B-E 7 string". That matches **B standard**, thickest to thinnest: B E A D G B E. Intervals: 5-5-5-5-4-5.

| String | Note | Gauge |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 7 (thickest) | B1 | .062 wound |
| 6 | E2 | .044 wound |
| 5 | A2 | .032 wound |
| 4 | D3 | .024 wound |
| 3 | G3 | .016 |
| 2 | B3 | .011 |
| 1 (thinnest) | E4 | .009 |

The page gives gauges only. The note names and octaves follow from "B standard" and the usual 7-string register.
