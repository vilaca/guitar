## Electronics and controls

- Two DiMarzio D Activator 7 humbuckers, passive with ceramic magnets.
- Controls: one volume knob, one tone knob and a three-way selector switch.
- Switching: neck position gives the neck pickup alone, centre gives both, bridge position gives the bridge pickup alone.
- Pots and capacitor (parts catalogue): volume B500K, tone A500K, tone capacitor 0.022 µF.

## Features, as the page describes them

Paraphrased from the page's feature blocks:

- **Neck:** a thin, flat, fast Wizard neck built from five pieces of maple and walnut, described as strong and playable.
- **Fretboard:** ebony, described as giving tight lows and mids, a strong high-end attack and rich sustain.
- **Body:** okoume, described as bright and solid sounding.
- **Pickups:** DiMarzio D Activator, described as powerful, clean and open, with tight low end.
- **Machine heads:** Gotoh, described as precise and smooth with minimal backlash.
- **Tremolo:** Edge-Zero II, a lower-profile tremolo with a stud-lock function for stable tuning.
- **Side dots:** luminescent, easy to see on dark stages.

### Note: nut radius R430 vs fretboard radius 400 mmR

- **The two figures:** the parts catalogue lists the locking nut as "R430" (430 mm, about 16.9"). The product page gives the fingerboard radius as "400mmR" (400 mm, about 15.75"). They differ by 30 mm, and the nut is the flatter of the two.
- **Not explained by either source:** neither says whether the fingerboard is a compound radius, whether 400 mmR is a nominal single figure, or why the nut radius is different. The product page lists one radius only.
- **General note, not from the sources:** a nut's radius normally follows the fingerboard radius at that point, and a 30 mm difference is small. It may be a catalogue rounding or a deliberate nut shape, but this can't be determined from the two documents.
- **How to settle it:** measure the fretboard at the nut and at the 12th and 24th frets with a radius gauge, and measure the nut slot curve. This matters mainly when replacing the nut or setting string height at the nut, so check the catalogue part number (2LN3YAA021) against the measured radius before ordering.
