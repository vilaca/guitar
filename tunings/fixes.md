# Fixes for the tunings reference

Review of `pdfs/guitar_tunings_discussed.pdf` (6 pages). The text is generated by `generators/generate_tunings_reference.py`, so make each fix there and regenerate.

All interval strings, transpositions, Phrygian roots and degrees, scale-membership tables, C-major uniqueness claims and the Homme G D realization were recalculated and are correct. Only the items below need changing.

## 1. Chat language in a standalone reference

- **Where:** title and intro (p1), "our Phrygian rule" (p3), "Other scales from the discussion" (p3), "worked realization used in our discussion" (p4).
- **Problem:** "our discussion" and "our Phrygian rule" mean nothing to someone reading the repo. "Guitar tunings discussed" has the same problem. The p3 note about "the scale guides" doesn't say where they are.
- **Fix:**
  - Retitle the PDF, for example "Guitar tunings reference". Rename the output files to match.
  - Reword the intro and the p3 and p4 phrases so they stand alone.
  - Point "the Phrygian rule" and "the scale guides" to the scale PDFs in `scales/pdfs/`.

## 2. p2: "7 custom drop E" has no listed base

- **Where:** "The all fourths examples" table, p2.
- **Problem:** `E B E A D G C` comes from dropping all-fourths `F# B E A D G C` by a whole tone. That base isn't listed. The listed base, `B E A D G C F`, gives drop A, not drop E.
- **Fix:** Add a row for `F# B E A D G C` (5-5-5-5-5-5), or add a sentence explaining where drop E comes from.

## 3. p5: citation for "standard eight-string tuning"

- **Where:** first paragraph, p5: "his standard eight-string tuning [3]".
- **Problem:** the 2013 article gives `F#-B-E-A-D-G-B-E` but never calls it standard. The 2016 article does.
- **Fix:** cite [3, 4]. Optionally add "doubled high string" wording that matches the 2013 article, which says only "a second .011 string" and doesn't say which string is doubled.

## 4. p4: string order is an assumption

- **Where:** first paragraph, p4.
- **Problem:** the forum post writes `GGDGDD` without spaces and gives no string order. "Thickest to thinnest" is the PDF's reading.
- **Fix:** add a clause such as "read here from thickest to thinnest".

## 5. p6: source URLs aren't visible in print

- **Where:** "Sources for the artist examples", p6.
- **Problem:** the sources are clickable links, but the page shows only labels. A printed copy has no way to reach them.
- **Fix:** print each URL, or a short form of it, under its label.

## 6. Layout and wording

- Each section starts a new page, so pages 1, 2, 3, 5 and 6 are about a third empty. Remove the forced breaks and the guide may fit in 4 pages.
- Hyphenation is inconsistent: "eight string" and "seven-string", "all fourths" and "all-fourths", "six string pitch realization". Pick one form.
- "The original eight string changes" (p1) is unclear without the chat. Try "Building the eight-string tuning".

## 7. Add the Xiphos (factory and current tuning) and the 29" baritone

Name the owner's seven-string instruments in the tables. All three tunings already appear in the p3 table, but none is tied to a guitar, and the p1 table lists only B standard for seven strings.

| Instrument | Tuning | Open notes | Semitones | Phrygian root |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Xiphos 7-string, factory tuning | A standard | A D G C F A D | 5-5-5-5-4-5 | A |
| Xiphos 7-string, current tuning | G standard | G C F Bb Eb G C | 5-5-5-5-4-5 | G |
| 29" 7-string baritone | B standard | B E A D G B E | 5-5-5-5-4-5 | B |

All three have the same interval pattern, so the same shapes and fret numbers work on each. Only the pitch changes.

- **p1, "Standard reference tunings":** the 7-string row is B standard only. Add "7 (A standard)" and "7 (G standard)" rows. Add an instrument column, or a note under the table, naming the Xiphos (A standard factory, G standard current) and the 29" baritone (B standard).
- **p3, "Lower tunings and open string scales":** relabel the rows "A standard (Xiphos factory)", "G standard (Xiphos current)" and "B standard (29-inch baritone)".
- **p3, "Other scales" table:** its "open pitches outside it" column assumes B standard or drop A. Add a second table covering A standard (`A D G C F A D`) and G standard (`G C F Bb Eb G C`), since the Xiphos has different open pitches. For the same scales, the open pitches outside each scale are:

| Scale | Notes | Outside it (A standard) | Outside it (G standard) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| G Phrygian | G Ab Bb C D Eb F | A | None |
| A Phrygian | A Bb C D E F G | None | Eb |
| B Phrygian | B C D E F# G A | F | F, Bb, Eb |
| E minor pentatonic | E G A B D | C, F | C, F, Bb, Eb |

  The G Phrygian row is the natural match for G standard, and A Phrygian for A standard. The other rows show what changes if you play the other scales on that tuning. These values and the 5-5-5-5-4-5 interval string were checked with a short script.
- **Link to the scale guides:** the guides in `scales/pdfs/` are written for B standard and drop A. On the Xiphos the same fret numbers work, but they sound lower: a whole tone lower in A standard (A Phrygian instead of B Phrygian), and a major third lower in G standard (G Phrygian). Say this once on p3, using the Phrygian PDF's rule: keep the fret numbers and apply the same root rule.
- **Where to change it:** add the new tunings to the tuning data in `generators/generate_tunings_reference.py`. Its assertions check the intervals and the Phrygian open-string rule.
- **Not verified:** the instrument labels and the tunings come from you. I haven't checked them against manufacturer specifications.
