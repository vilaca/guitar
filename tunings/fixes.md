# Tuning reference review decisions

The [six-page tuning reference](pdfs/guitar_tunings_reference.pdf) and the editable Word version are built from the [same source](generators/generate_tunings_reference.py).

| Review item | Status | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Standalone title, headings and scale-guide reference | Applied | The title and output filenames now say “Guitar tunings reference.” The explanations stand on their own, and page 3 links directly to the B-standard Phrygian tab. |
| 2. Starting tuning for custom drop E | Applied | Page 2 lists F# B E A D G C. Lowering only the seventh string from F# to E gives E B E A D G C. |
| 3. Carpenter attribution | Applied | Page 5 cites the 2013 rig rundown for the pitches and the 2016 interview for Carpenter's description of standard eight-string tuning, drop E and the high-E drone. |
| 4. Homme string order and octaves | Applied | Page 4 identifies GGDGDD as a viewer transcription. Reading it from thickest to thinnest and assigning specific octaves are choices made for the worked example. |
| 5. Printable artist sources | Applied | Page 6 prints each full URL under its source label. The links also work in the PDF and Word outputs. |
| 6. Consistent wording and hyphenation | Applied | The eight-string example has a descriptive heading, and compound modifiers such as “seven-string” and “all-fourths” are hyphenated. |
| 6. Compress the guide to four pages | Not applied | The six-page arrangement keeps each artist example on one page and the expanded tuning and scale tables together on page 3. |
| 7. Instrument labels and tuning provenance | Applied | Pages 1 and 3 identify the Solar in B standard and the Xiphos in factory A standard and owner-reported G standard. The instrument sheets document the source of each tuning. |
| 7. Xiphos open-string membership | Applied | Page 3 calculates the outside notes for each tuning across six scales, including B minor pentatonic and B♭ dominant bebop. A shared table lets readers compare the instruments directly. |
| 7. Transposing the published tab | Applied | Page 3 explains how the B-standard fingering produces A Phrygian or G Phrygian on the Xiphos. |

## Source notes

- The viewer reports the note sequence around 52 minutes into the interview. The video excerpt provides listening context. The worked octave assignments come from the guide. [Viewer transcription](https://www.guitarristas.info/foros/josh-homme-echopark-signature/349903) · [Video excerpt](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NE81yc52qto)
- The 2013 article lists the seven- and eight-string pitches. In 2016 Carpenter describes the factory eight-string tuning, dropping the bass to E, and using an extra high E as a drone. [2013 rig rundown](https://www.premierguitar.com/rig-rundown-deftones-stephen-carpenter-sergio-vega) · [2016 interview](https://www.premierguitar.com/artists/deftones-a-little-darkness)
- The Xiphos sheet distinguishes its factory tuning from the owner's current setup. The Solar sheet explains the manufacturer's “B-E 7 string” designation. [Xiphos](../guitars/ibanez-xptb720.md) · [Solar](../guitars/solar-a1.7rop-29.md)
